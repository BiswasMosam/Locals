# Profile template

Every job in the town gets one profile with these sections, in this order. Tag every line with its source (`[I]` `[O]` `[D]` `[A]` `[P]` `[G]`, see the [study rules](README.md#rules-of-the-study)).

## 1. Snapshot
Three or four sentences: where they work, what a good day looks like, what a bad day costs them.

## 2. The day
A timeline from waking to sleeping, one row per block of time.

| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

"Hands and eyes" is the column apps forget: one hand on a handlebar, oily fingers, glare, a customer watching.

## 3. The week, the month, the year
Cycles that change the load: market days, month end, festival season, monsoon, exam season, court vacations.

## 4. Tools today
What they use now and **why it survives**. The paper diary, the WhatsApp group, the calculator, the memory of a regular's usual order. An app has to beat these, so name what each one is good at.

## 5. Phone and ability
Phone class and age, screen size, storage, language settings, font size, reading habits, typing or voice notes, eyesight, who they ask when stuck.

## 6. Pressures
What costs money, time or face. What makes them drop a tool for good.

## 7. Their words
The words they use for the things in their work, next to the words an app might use instead.

| They say | An app might say |
|---|---|

## 8. Three different people
At least three people in this job who differ in age, size of business, tech comfort and language. Never one average person.

## 9. Task cards
Real jobs they would bring to an app, each with the pressure it happens under and how we check it was done.

| Task | Pressure | Done when |
|---|---|---|

## 10. Limits in numbers
The numbers a local's code enforces. Each one cites its evidence.

| Limit | Value | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Patience before giving up on a step | | |
| How much of a label they read | | |
| Slip rate (wrong tap, typo) | | |
| What they do when stuck (ask, retry, workaround, quit) | | |

## 11. Caricature traps
What a model would wrongly assume about this person, written down so we can test for it.

## 12. Open questions
One question for every `[G]`, ready for the interview.
