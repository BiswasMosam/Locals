# Fresh graduate applying for jobs

**Status:** draft, from a desk study and from building [Fill.ai](https://www.mosambiswas.com/Fill.ai/). Ring one: the people Fill.ai is for. The town's final year students play them (the [students](students/README.md) family), on the laptop where job forms are filled.

## 1. Snapshot
A final year student or a new graduate filling the same details into form after form: placement drives on Google Forms, company career pages, job portals `[P]`. Graduates face the hardest market in the country: the India Employment Report 2024 put unemployment among graduates at 29.1%, 34.5% for women [1] `[D]`. In a survey of 2,633 job seekers, 70% said a first job is harder to get than three to five years ago, and 61% said they rarely hear back after applying [2] `[D]`. A good day is ten applications sent with no mistakes; a bad day is a form that cleared itself on a wrong date format `[P]`.

## 3. The week, the month, the year
Campus placement season runs through the final year; off campus hiring and internship cycles follow results `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
The résumé PDF, a notes file of answers to paste, the browser's autofill, Google Forms, job portals `[P]`. Autofill survives because it is instant; it fails on custom questions, dropdowns and date formats `[P]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Comfortable with apps; fills long forms on a laptop and quick ones on the phone `[G]`. Reads the field labels, skims the instructions `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Volume (many forms a week), deadlines, and fear of a wrong answer going to an employer `[D]` `[G]`. Drops a tool that fills something wrong or asks too much setup before it helps `[G]`.

Each local keeps their own limits ([limits](../limits.md)); the task runs on a laptop, because Fill.ai is a desktop browser extension.

## 11. Caricature traps
- **"Young people get every app at once."** Comfort with apps is not patience with a setup screen.
- **"Graduates are desperate."** The numbers say the market is hard; how each person feels about it is theirs.
