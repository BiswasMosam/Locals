# Toy car collector

**Status:** draft, from a desk study and from building [Peghunt](https://github.com/BiswasMosam/Peghunt). Ring one: the people Peghunt is for. No locals yet: Peghunt is a camera app, and an emulator has no store wall to point it at (see open questions).

## 1. Snapshot
Someone who walks into a toy store, a supermarket or a stall and scans the wall of Hot Wheels cards for the few worth hunting `[P]`. A Super Treasure Hunt shares its card and number with the regular car beside it; what gives it away is Spectraflame paint, Real Riders rubber tyres and a small TH mark [1] `[A]` `[P]`. Collector guides put it at about one Super Treasure Hunt in a case of 72 cars [2] `[A]`, a ratio Mattel does not publish `[G]`. A good day is a find before another hunter; a bad day is a wall already picked over `[A]` `[G]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restock day | A store aisle | Scanning a wall of cards `[P]` | Phone in one hand, cards in the other `[P]` | Camera, a checklist `[P]` | Staff, other hunters `[A]` `[G]` |
| Evening | Home | Sorting finds, checking lists, groups `[A]` `[G]` | Free `[G]` | Collector groups `[A]` `[G]` | None `[G]` |

## 3. The week, the month, the year
New cases arrive on a store's restock day, and each year's series changes the cars to look for `[A]` `[P]`.

## 4. Tools today
Memory, a printed or phone checklist, collector groups and guides `[A]` `[P]`. Memory survives because the aisle is no place to read a list; it fails on a wall of a hundred near identical cards `[P]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Uses the phone camera in poor store light, at arm's length, with glare on the blister packs `[P]`.

## 6. Pressures
Speed (someone else may get there first), certainty before paying, and the store's patience `[A]` `[G]`.
