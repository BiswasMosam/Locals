# Recruiter reading a portfolio

**Status:** draft. Ring one: the people a developer's portfolio is for. The jobs themselves are drafted in the [business](business/README.md) family ([HR recruiter](business/hr-recruiter.md), [hiring manager](business/hiring-manager.md), [founder CEO](business/founder-ceo.md)); this page is the situation they meet a portfolio in, and the town's own people play it.

## 1. Snapshot
A candidate's name has come up, and their site is open between other things. Recruiters spend seconds, not minutes, on a first look: an eye tracking study by Ladders put the first screen of a résumé at 7.4 seconds on average in 2018, up from about six in 2012, with job titles and section headings drawing the eye and cluttered layouts and long sentences doing badly [1] `[D]` (the study's method is not published in detail, so treat the number as a scale, not a measurement). A good visit ends with the résumé or the code open; a bad one ends on a loading screen `[G]`.

## 2. The day
Their working days are in the business family's profiles. The visit itself: between calls, on a laptop at the desk or a phone in a cab `[G]`.
