# Law

Lawyers in the town, split into four types by **how differently their day runs**. Practice area (criminal, family, corporate, tax) does not split a type on its own: a criminal lawyer and a family lawyer at the same district court live nearly the same day, so they appear as different people inside one type.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial court advocate | District and sessions courts, own chamber | The cause list and the next date | [Draft](trial-advocate.md) |
| Junior advocate | Corridors and registries, a senior's chamber | Being sent: mention this, file that, note the date | [Draft](junior-advocate.md) |
| Law firm associate | A firm office, a laptop, email | Partners, deals and timesheets | [Draft](firm-associate.md) |
| In-house counsel | A company office, chat and email | Colleagues who need it signed today | [Draft](in-house-counsel.md) |

## What the whole family shares

- **A huge, slow system.** District and subordinate courts held 4,76,57,328 pending cases on 31 December 2025 `[D]`. Frequent adjournments are named by the government as a cause of delay, and "lawyer unavailable" was the recorded reason for 66,59,565 delayed district court cases by September 2024 `[D]`. A lawyer's calendar is a pile of dates that keep moving.
- **Many lawyers, few women at the top.** About 1.7 million advocates nationally by the Chief Justice's figure `[D]`. In the 15 state bar councils that report gender, women are about 18% of enrolled advocates `[D]`.
- **Phones are already part of the work.** The government's eCourts Services app tracks a case by its 16 character CNR number, shows cause lists and an advocate's dated case list, and reported 57 lakh downloads `[D]`. The Gujarat High Court sends advocates their own daily cause list on WhatsApp every evening `[D]`. Lawyers coordinate filings and briefs with colleagues on WhatsApp `[A]`.
- **Confidentiality.** Clients' documents are privileged. Advocates may not advertise or solicit work under Bar Council rules `[D]`, which shapes how they use messaging and social apps.
