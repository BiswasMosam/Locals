# Trial court advocate

**Status:** draft from published sources. Family context in [Law](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot

An advocate with an established practice in a district and sessions court complex, with a chamber, a few juniors and a steady stream of clients `[G]`. The day belongs to the cause list: which of their matters are listed, in which courtroom, at roughly what time `[D]`. A good day is several matters heard and moved forward. A bad day is two matters called at once in different courtrooms, or a missed date that hurts a client `[G]`.

## 2. The day

Court hours vary by complex; one Maharashtra district court lists 10:45 to 5:15 with lunch from 1:30 to 2:00 `[A]` (unofficial listing).

| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Night before | Home or chamber | Reads tomorrow's cause list, pulls the files `[D]` `[G]` | Free | eCourts app or website, WhatsApp cause list where offered `[D]` | Client calls `[G]` |
| 9:30 | Chamber | Briefs juniors on who covers which courtroom `[G]` | Files in hand | WhatsApp to juniors `[G]` | Clients arriving early `[G]` |
| 10:45 to 1:30 | Courtrooms | Waits for matters to be called, argues, seeks passovers when clashing `[A]` | Standing, gown, files, crowded halls `[A]` | Silent; glances only `[G]` | A junior's message that another matter is being called `[G]` |
| 1:30 | Bar room or chamber | Lunch, quick client meetings `[G]` | Free | Catching up on messages `[G]` | Many `[G]` |

| Evening | Chamber | Client conferences, drafting, dictation to a typist `[G]` | Seated | Calls, documents arriving on WhatsApp `[G]` | Walk in clients `[G]` |

## 3. The week, the month, the year

- Court vacations, typically in summer and around Diwali, change the load `[G]`.
- Dates cluster unpredictably; one adjournment moves a matter weeks or months `[D]`.

## 4. Tools today

| Tool | What it is good at |
|---|---|
| A paper date diary `[G]` | Every matter's next date in one place, readable in a courtroom without a screen |
| eCourts Services app `[D]` | Case status by CNR, cause lists, an advocate's date wise case list |
| WhatsApp `[A]` | Juniors, clients and colleagues, documents and photos |
| Typist and photocopy shops in the complex `[G]` | Fast drafting and copies on the spot |
| A research database, if subscribed `[G]` | Judgments, often expensive |

## 5. Phone and ability

- Uses a phone constantly but briefly; long reading happens on paper `[G]`.
- Reads English legal text fluently; talks to clients in the local language `[G]`.
- Juniors handle much of the phone admin `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures

- Two matters called at once in different courtrooms `[A]`.
- A missed date can cost a client their case `[G]`.
- Clients expect updates the same day `[G]`.
- Reputation: clients choose advocates they have seen before `[A]`.

## 9. Task cards

| Task | Pressure | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Find tomorrow's listed matters and which courtroom each is in. | Night before, tired | A list of matters with courtrooms is on screen |
| A matter is adjourned. Record the new date. | Next matter about to be called | The new date is saved against the right matter |
| A client asks on WhatsApp what happened today. Send an update. | Five other clients waiting | The update is sent to the right client |
| Find the last order in a matter while standing in the corridor. | Judge may call it any minute | The order is open on screen |
| Hand tomorrow's courtroom to a junior with the file details. | End of day | The junior has the matter, court and time |

## 11. Caricature traps

- **Television courtroom drama.** Most of the day is waiting, mentioning and getting dates, not speeches.
- **"Lawyers read every word."** In court they glance; careful reading happens at night.
- **"Lawyers avoid technology."** The eCourts app has tens of lakhs of downloads `[D]`; the limits are a crowded courtroom and a silent phone, not unwillingness.
- **Argumentative narration.** A local should sound like a busy professional, not a cartoon of one.
