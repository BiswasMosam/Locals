# Junior advocate

**Status:** draft from published sources. Family context in [Law](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot

An advocate in the first years of practice, attached to a senior's chamber in a district court complex `[A]`. Much of the work is being sent: mention a matter and ask for a pass over or an adjournment, file papers at the registry, note the next date `[A]`. Pay is uncertain; juniors without family backing may get no basic pay at all `[A]`, and the Bar Council's recommended stipend of ₹20,000 a month in cities and ₹15,000 in rural areas is guidance, not law `[D]`. A good day is every errand done and a chance to argue. A bad day is a filing returned for a small defect, or a public correction in court `[A]`.

## 3. The week, the month, the year

- Filing deadlines drive panic days, and limitation periods are fixed by statute: an appeal from a decree must be filed within 90 days to a High Court and 30 days to any other court (Limitation Act 1963, article 116), and filing late needs an application to excuse the delay (section 5) `[D]`.
- Court vacations (in 2026 at the Bombay High Court: 11 May to 7 June, 6 to 18 November, 25 December to 3 January `[D]`) are when juniors catch up or look for paid work `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability

- Very comfortable with apps; grew up on a smartphone `[G]`.
- Often a budget Android phone with a full gallery of file photos `[G]`.
- Mobile data in a court basement or crowded corridor can fail `[G]`. The eCourts app shows a connection error when it cannot refresh `[D]`.

## 6. Pressures

- Filings returned for minor clerical or formatting defects, more often to unfamiliar faces `[A]`.
- Files that sit at the registry for days with no way to chase them `[A]`.
- Some chambers do not let juniors sign a vakalatnama or appear on record `[A]`.
- Money: commuting, copies and court fees paid out of pocket `[G]`.
- Women juniors face added barriers around safety, late hours and informal networks `[A]`.

## 11. Caricature traps

- **"Juniors are clueless."** They run the logistics of an entire practice under pressure.
- **"Young means patient with bad apps."** Young users drop slow apps fastest; they simply have more options.
- **Self pity in narration.** The strain is real `[A]`, but a local should describe it plainly, not dramatically.
