# Law firm associate

**Status:** draft from published sources and job descriptions. Family context in [Law](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot

A lawyer at a corporate law firm in a city business district, working on transactions or disputes for companies `[G]`. The work is drafting, reviewing and negotiating agreements, running due diligence and preparing reports, under partners' supervision `[A]` (job descriptions). Long, unsustainable hours are the main reason associates leave, according to a survey of over 1,450 professionals at over 300 Indian firms `[D]`. A good day is a draft turned around before the deadline. A bad day is a midnight email that undoes the day's work `[G]`.

## 11. Caricature traps

- **"Corporate lawyers love long documents."** They read them because they must, and want tools that get them to the right page.
- **Glamour.** Most of the day is email, spreadsheets and redlines.
- **Endless patience at night.** Tired users make mistakes; the local should too.
