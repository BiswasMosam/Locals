# Business

The people who decide: founders and chief executives, the investors who back them, and the recruiters and hiring managers who choose who joins. In the town they mostly sit across a table, so the [rooms](../../rooms.md) put them in boardrooms, pitch halls and interview panels. Split into five types by **how differently their day runs**.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder CEO | Customer calls, the team, the next round | Runway, the pipeline, the board update | [Draft](founder-ceo.md) |
| Company CEO or managing director | The cabin, back to back meetings | Margins, reviews, a long list of decisions | [Draft](company-ceo.md) |
| Investor | Decks, founder calls, partner meetings | Finding the few worth backing | [Draft](investor.md) |
| HR recruiter | Drives, screening calls, offers | How many hired by when | [Draft](hr-recruiter.md) |
| Hiring manager | Their own team's work, plus interviews | Someone who can do the job soon | [Draft](hiring-manager.md) |

## What the family shares

- **Time is the scarce thing.** In a study of 27 chief executives whose assistants logged every 15 minutes for 13 weeks, meetings took 72% of their working time, about 37 a week, most often an hour long; the CEOs themselves said one hour meetings could often be cut to 30 or even 15 minutes `[D]` [1].
- **Decks are read fast.** Investors spent 3 minutes 44 seconds on a pitch deck on average in 2015 `[D]` [2] and about 2 minutes 44 seconds in 2021 `[D]` [3]; decks that went on to raise money held them for over four minutes, decks that did not for about a minute and a half `[D]` [4].
- **The team comes first.** Of 885 venture capitalists surveyed, 95% named the management team as an important factor and 47% as the most important, ahead of the business model (83% important), the product (74%) and the market (68%) `[D]` [5].
- **Interviewers decide early, but not as early as the myth.** Across more than 600 real interviews, 4.9% of decisions came in the first minute, 25.5% within five minutes and 59.9% within fifteen; structured interviews slowed decisions down `[D]` [6].
- **Structure makes interviews fairer.** Rating each answer on anchored scales, by more than one interviewer, is among the components that make interviews predict job performance better: validities of .35 to .62 for structured interviews against .14 to .33 for unstructured ones in one review `[D]` [7].
- **A lot of founders.** India had 1,97,692 startups recognised by DPIIT on 31 October 2025 `[D]` [8].
