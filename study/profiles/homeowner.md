# Household hiring a fabricator

**Status:** draft, from a desk study and from building [Pops](../../tasks/pops.toml) for a real fabrication shop. Ring one: the people who visit the [Biswas Fabricators site](https://biswasfabricators.com/). This is a situation, not a job, so the town's own people play it: a teacher, a nurse, an insurance agent, anyone with a flat and a problem at the window.

## 1. Snapshot
A family in a flat in Mumbai, Thane or Navi Mumbai wants something fitted: a grill, an invisible grill, a mosquito or bird net, a sliding door, a terrace shed `[P]`. The shop works by phone: the visitor calls, the shop visits, measures, quotes, then fits `[P]`. A good day for the visitor is a call answered and a visit fixed; a bad day is a page that does not say whether the shop comes to their area, or a number that does not answer, and the job goes to whoever the neighbour knew `[G]`.

| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |

| Morning rush | Home | Getting children and themselves out `[G]` | Both busy `[G]` | Searching in a spare minute `[G]` | Everything `[G]` |
| Commute | Train or bus | Searching, comparing shops `[G]` | One hand, standing `[G]` | The main search time `[G]` | Stations `[G]` |
| Evening | Home, at the balcony | Looking at the problem, deciding to call `[G]` | Free `[G]` | Calls the shop `[G]` | Family `[G]` |

## 3. The week, the month, the year
Pigeon trouble is a city-wide issue: in July 2025 the BMC began closing 51 pigeon feeding places over health hazards from droppings, and the Bombay High Court kept the ban on feeding while asking hospitals for data on lung illness linked to droppings [1] `[D]`. Netting and grills are bought before the monsoon and before festivals, when families get the house ready `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
A neighbour's word, the society's WhatsApp group, a search on the phone, and the number on a shop board `[G]`. The phone call survives because the shop has to see the balcony before it can quote `[P]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
India had 886 million active internet users in 2024, 397 million of them urban, and the phone is the main way people get online in both cities and villages [2] `[D]`; one in five used a shared device and one in five used voice commands [2] `[D]`. Reads a business page for three things: what they make, whether they come here, how to call `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Health and safety at the window (droppings, children near an open balcony) `[D]` `[G]`; a fair price; a shop that will turn up `[G]`. Drops a site that hides the phone number or never names the areas it serves `[G]`.

## 8. Three different people
Played by the town's own people. A household is not a job, so who plays it changes what they bring: a rushed class teacher between school and home, a nurse after a night shift, an insurance agent who reads every line.

## 10. Limits in numbers
Each local keeps their own limits ([limits](../limits.md)); the situation adds none.

## 11. Caricature traps
- **"Homeowners read the whole page."** A visitor is looking for three things and leaves when they do not find them.
- **"Everyone in Mumbai wants invisible grills."** The evidence is about pigeons and health; what a family buys depends on the flat and the budget.
