# Education

People who teach the town, split into four types by **how differently their day runs**: a government school with forty portals on top of the teaching, a private school with parents in every group, a college short of permanent teachers, and coaching classes that run on entrance exam dates. Subjects and seniority become the different people inside a type.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government school teacher | A municipal or zilla parishad school | Teaching, plus apps, portals and election duty | [Draft](govt-school-teacher.md) |
| Private school teacher | A private school, then the parents' group | The syllabus, the diary and parents' messages | [Draft](private-school-teacher.md) |
| College lecturer | A degree college, often more than one | Lectures by the hour, exams and accreditation files | [Draft](college-lecturer.md) |
| Coaching teacher | Coaching classes, mornings and evenings | JEE and NEET dates, test series and doubts | [Draft](coaching-teacher.md) |

## What the family shares

- **The school year is fixed by the state.** Maharashtra's HSC exams ran from 10 February to 11 March 2026 and SSC from 20 February to 18 March `[D]`; summer vacation runs into mid June `[A]`; Diwali brings about two weeks off `[A]` (school calendars differ).
- **Non teaching work is a burden.** Maharashtra teachers' associations complained of about 40 state apps and portals in December 2025 and asked for one portal and a data entry assistant `[A]`; teachers rallied at Azad Maidan against Booth Level Officer duties `[A]` and called a statewide shutdown on 5 December 2025 that included an end to online and non academic work among its demands `[A]`.
- **Permanent posts are empty.** In Maharashtra's government aided colleges, 11,918 of 31,185 sanctioned teaching posts were vacant at the end of 2024; colleges fill the gap with teachers paid by the clock hour `[D]`.
- **Coaching runs on exam dates.** JEE Main 2026 sat in January and April and NEET UG on 3 May `[D]`. Ministry of Education guidelines from 2024 ask states to stop coaching centres enrolling students under 16 `[D]`.
