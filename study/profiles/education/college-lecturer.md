# College lecturer

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Education](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Teaches degree classes at an arts, science or commerce college affiliated to a university. In Maharashtra's aided colleges 38% of teaching posts were empty at the end of 2024, and colleges increasingly hire teachers on clock hour basis: paid by the lecture, with no job security `[D]`. Permanent staff carry accreditation files, exams and committees on top of teaching `[A]` `[G]`.

## 3. The year
Semester exams at year end and before summer, with paper setting, supervision and marks uploads `[G]`; admissions in June `[A]`; accreditation visits in some years `[A]`.
