# Government school teacher

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Education](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Teaches at a municipal or zilla parishad school, often more than one class at once, and carries the state's data work: attendance, scholarships, midday meals, surveys, election rolls `[A]`. Teachers' associations counted about 40 state apps and portals in 2025 and said the data work cuts into teaching `[A]`. A good day is a lesson finished. A bad one is a portal deadline and a BLO list on the same afternoon `[A]` `[G]`.

## 2. The day

## 3. The year
School starts in mid June; board exams in February and March bring invigilation `[D]`; vacations still carry data work `[A]` `[G]`; election and census years add duties: the Right to Education Act bars using teachers for non educational work except the ten yearly census, disaster relief and elections to local bodies, the state legislature and Parliament (section 27) `[D]`, and courts have held election work may start before the poll is notified `[D]`.

## 4. Tools today
Paper registers, the state's education apps and portals, WhatsApp groups from the block office, a personal phone `[A]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Capable on the apps they are made to use, tired of new ones; reads in Marathi or Hindi first, English labels slow them down `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Data deadlines, non teaching duties, large classes, posts left empty `[A]` `[D]`.
