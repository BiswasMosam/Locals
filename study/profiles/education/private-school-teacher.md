# Private school teacher

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Education](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Teaches at a private school with a class of their own, and keeps parents informed through the school app and class WhatsApp groups, a practice found mostly in private schools `[A]`. Some teachers say they are overwhelmed by messages, especially in many or large groups `[A]`. A good day is a class that listened. A bad one is a parent's angry message at ten at night `[G]`.
