# Coaching teacher

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Education](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Teaches at coaching classes for entrance exams or school subjects: batches before and after school, test series, doubts at all hours `[A]` `[G]`. The year is set by JEE Main (January and April 2026) and NEET UG (3 May 2026) `[D]`. Ministry of Education guidelines from 2024 ask states to stop coaching centres enrolling students under 16, require tutors to be graduates and forbid promising ranks `[D]`.

## 3. The year
The weeks before JEE and NEET are the most intense `[D]` `[G]`; board exams in February and March bring revision batches `[D]` `[G]`; new batches start after results `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
WhatsApp groups per batch, YouTube or a classes app for videos, a phone camera for solutions, test software `[A]` `[G]`.
