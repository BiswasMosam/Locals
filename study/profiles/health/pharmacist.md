# Pharmacist

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Health](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Works the counter of a chemist's shop or a hospital pharmacy: reading prescriptions, finding the medicine, billing, ordering stock `[A]` `[G]`. The law asks for a registered pharmacist at every retail counter `[D]`, yet in one survey only 31% of consumers could regularly get help from a qualified pharmacist and 64% said their chemist rarely or never asked for a prescription `[D]`.
