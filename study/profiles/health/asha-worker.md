# ASHA worker

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Health](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
An Accredited Social Health Activist: a woman from the community who visits homes for pregnancies, newborns, vaccinations, TB and other programmes, and reports it all `[A]`. ASHAs keep a paper diary and then enter the same details into several apps `[A]`, retry failed uploads, and ask their children to help with data entry `[A]`, often on their own phones and data `[A]`. One study found ASHAs logging in on 88% of working days when an app fit their work `[D]`.

## 3. The year
Monsoon fevers bring surveys and follow ups `[D]` `[G]`; campaigns come in waves; in the 2020 lockdown ASHAs did door to door surveys `[A]`.

## 4. Tools today
Paper diaries and registers, several government apps, WhatsApp groups with supervisors `[A]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Often a low end phone, small text, shared with family; skilled at the apps she is trained on, unsure with new ones `[A]` `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Too many apps, incentives paid late, uploads that fail `[A]`.
