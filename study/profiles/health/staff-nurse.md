# Staff nurse

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Health](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Cares for patients on a ward or in an ICU in rotating shifts: medicines, vitals, dressings, the handover and the charting `[A]` `[G]`. Indian Nursing Council norms ask for one nurse to three general ward beds in medical colleges and one to one in ICU `[D]`; Maharashtra's public facilities are 35% short of nurses `[D]`, and nurses struck in July 2025 `[D]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shift start | Nursing station | Handover, the shift's list `[A]` | Charts, the ward computer `[G]` | None `[G]` | Doctors' rounds `[G]` |
| Shift | Ward or ICU | Medicines, vitals, dressings `[A]` | Gloves on, hands busy `[G]` | Pocket only, for urgent calls `[G]` | Every bell `[G]` |
| Shift end | Nursing station | Charting, indents `[A]` `[G]` | Ward computer or registers `[G]` | A break, finally `[G]` | Late admissions `[G]` |

## 3. The year
Monsoon fevers fill wards `[D]`; night shift rotations change sleep every few weeks `[G]`; strikes over pay and posts `[D]`.

## 4. Tools today
Paper charts and registers, hospital systems in larger hospitals, WhatsApp groups for the ward `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Practical and careful with numbers; phone use is in short gaps, often with gloves just off `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Patients per nurse; staff who do not turn up; documentation after the shift `[D]` `[G]`.
