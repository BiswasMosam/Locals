# Resident doctor

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Health](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
A doctor in postgraduate training at a teaching hospital, running wards, casualty and the operation theatre under seniors `[A]`. In FAIMA's 2026 survey of 1,260 residents, 46.7% said they work more than 80 hours a week, about 62% had worked past 36 hours at a stretch, and 63.7% rarely or never got rest after duty `[D]` (online and self selected, not a national sample).
