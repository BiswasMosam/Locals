# Engineering

Engineers in the town, split into eight types by **how differently their day runs**: at a desk, on a shop floor, on a construction site, at a lab bench. Specialities become the different people inside a type.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software engineer, product company | A desk, chat and alerts | Sprints, reviews and the on-call pager | [Draft](software-product.md) |
| Software engineer, IT services | An office or home, a client abroad | The client's hours and the timesheet | [Draft](software-services.md) |
| Mechanical, plant maintenance | The shop floor, in shifts | Breakdowns, permits and the logbook | [Draft](plant-maintenance.md) |
| Mechanical, design | A CAD workstation | Drawings, revisions and the shop floor's questions | [Draft](mechanical-design.md) |
| Civil, site engineer | A construction site in the sun or rain | Labour, materials and the daily progress report | [Draft](site-engineer.md) |
| Civil, structural and planning | An office with drawings and schedules | Approvals, clashes and dates | [Draft](structural-planning.md) |
| Electrical, maintenance | Panels, substations and plant floors | Trips, safety permits and shift handovers | [Draft](electrical-maintenance.md) |
| Electronics, embedded | A lab bench with boards and probes | Firmware, test rigs and a board that will not boot | [Draft](embedded-engineer.md) |

## What the family shares

- **Reports go up every day.** Site engineers send morning and evening progress reports in a set format to a WhatsApp group, with labour counts, materials used and site photos `[A]` (job postings). Site photos and inspection notes get lost in personal WhatsApp chats `[A]`.
- **The plant runs on permits and logs.** Maintenance engineers run toolbox talks, issue work permits and lockout tags, fill daily checklists and breakdown registers, and close work orders in SAP `[A]` (job postings).
- **Software hours stretch across time zones.** A quarter of 1,450 Indian IT professionals surveyed by Blind in March 2025 said they work more than 70 hours a week `[D]`; a 2025 study of time zone shifts reports sleep disruption and fatigue `[D]`. Early standups and late client calls are common accounts `[A]`.
- **Most job details here come from job postings**, which describe duties, not days. Every timeline is `[G]` until someone in the job corrects it.
