# Mechanical, plant maintenance

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Engineering](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Keeps a plant running in shifts: toolbox talks, work permits and lockouts, rounds of the equipment, fixing breakdowns before they stop production, and logging everything `[A]` (job postings). A good shift is one with no stoppage. A bad shift is a line down and the production head on the phone `[G]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shift start | Maintenance room | Toolbox talk, allocating manpower `[A]` | Clipboard `[G]` | Calls `[G]` | Handover issues `[G]` |
| Shift | Shop floor | Rounds, breakdowns, permits `[A]` | Gloves, grease, noise `[G]` | Calls over machine noise `[G]` | Breakdowns `[A]` |
| Shift end | Desk | Breakdown register, closing work orders in SAP `[A]` | Shared computer `[G]` | Little `[G]` | Next shift `[G]` |

## 3. The year
Planned shutdowns, often around Diwali, are the busiest maintenance weeks `[G]`; monsoon brings leaks and electrical trouble `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
Logbooks, checklists, SAP PM, permit forms, WhatsApp groups for the shift `[A]` `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Uses the phone in short bursts, often with gloves or dirty hands `[G]`. Works shifts under factory law: an adult worker's day is at most 9 hours and the week 48, spread over no more than 10.5 hours, with overtime paid at twice the rate (Factories Act 1948, sections 51, 54, 56, 59) `[D]`.

## 6. Pressures
Downtime; safety; parts not in store `[A]` `[G]`.
