# Civil, site engineer

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Engineering](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Runs the work on a construction site: allocates labour and contractors, checks measurements and contractor bills, and sends daily progress reports with labour counts, materials and photos, morning and evening, to a WhatsApp group `[A]` (job postings). A good day is the slab poured and the DPR sent. A bad day is rain, missing material and labour gone home `[G]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8:00 | Site | Allocating work to contractors and labour `[A]` | Helmet, drawings `[G]` | Morning report on WhatsApp `[A]` | Labour shortages `[G]` |
| Day | Site, floors and scaffolds | Checking work, measurements `[A]` | Sun on the screen, dust `[G]` | Photos for the report `[A]` | Material deliveries `[G]` |
| Evening | Site office cabin | Evening report, bills `[A]` | Tired `[G]` | WhatsApp group `[A]` | Calls from the PMC `[A]` |

## 3. The year
Monsoon slows or stops work; labour goes home to villages around festivals; summer heat `[G]`.
