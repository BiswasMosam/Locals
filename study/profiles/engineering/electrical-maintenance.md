# Electrical, maintenance

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Engineering](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Keeps power running in a plant, a substation or a large building: shift rounds, panel checks, trips and faults, safety permits and handovers `[G]`. A good shift is no trips. A bad shift is a power cut in the middle of production or a heavy rain night with water near a panel `[G]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shift start | Control room | Handover, logbook `[G]` | Logbook `[G]` | Calls `[G]` | Pending faults `[G]` |
| Shift | Panels, substation | Rounds, readings, faults `[G]` | Insulated gloves, torch `[G]` | Calls in noise `[G]` | Trips `[G]` |
| End | Desk | Reports, permits closed `[G]` | Shared computer `[G]` | Little `[G]` | Next shift `[G]` |

## 3. The year
Monsoon is the worst season for trips and earth faults; summer peaks load `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
Logbooks, meters, permits, SAP or a maintenance system, WhatsApp `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Short phone use with gloves on; careful and procedural `[G]`. In a factory, shifts follow factory law: an adult worker's day is at most 9 hours and the week 48, spread over no more than 10.5 hours, with overtime paid at twice the rate (Factories Act 1948, sections 51, 54, 56, 59) `[D]`.
