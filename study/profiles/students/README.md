# Students

College students: the audience in most of the town's classrooms and the people giving most of its presentations. Split into two types by **how differently their day runs**: engineering colleges, with practicals, projects and placements, and commerce colleges, with lectures, internals and often a part time job.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering student | Lectures, labs, the project group | Submissions, internal marks, placements | [Draft](engineering-student.md) |
| Commerce student | Lectures, the library, often a part time job | Internals, assignments, a career after | [Draft](commerce-student.md) |

## What the family shares

- **Many students, most in a first degree.** India's higher education had 4.33 crore students in 2021 to 22, 78.9% of them in undergraduate programmes; of undergraduates, 34.2% studied arts, 14.8% science, 13.3% commerce and 11.8% engineering and technology `[D]` [1].
- **Attention in a lecture comes and goes.** Students report short lapses, mostly a minute or less, that come more often as a lecture goes on; demonstrations and questions to the class are followed by fewer lapses `[D]` [2]. Mind wandering rises and memory of the material falls the longer a lecture runs `[D]` [3] [4].
- **Young minds wander more.** In daily life, young adults reported mind wandering on 41% of probes against 28% for older adults `[D]` [5].
- **They read at the rate most reading studies measured.** College students are the people most reading research tested, so they take the town's graduate reading rate ([limits](../../limits.md)) `[D]`.
