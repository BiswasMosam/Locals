# Engineering student

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Students](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Studies for a four year engineering degree at a college affiliated to a university: lectures, practicals with journals to submit, a final year project in a group, and campus placements in the last year `[A]` `[G]`. Engineering and technology were 11.8% of India's undergraduates in 2021 to 22 `[D]`.

## 3. The year
Semester exams twice a year; internal assessments and submissions before them; placements in the final year `[A]` `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
College portals, Google Classroom, WhatsApp groups, a laptop if they have one `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Skims fast; reads properly when marks depend on it `[G]`. Some came from schools taught in another language, and long English sentences slow them down `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Attendance rules: under the University of Mumbai's ordinance a learner needs 75% attendance on average and at least 50% in each course to sit the exam, and some colleges ask 75% in every subject `[D]`; internal marks, a backlog to clear, and placements `[A]` `[G]`.
