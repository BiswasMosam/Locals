# Insurance agent

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Finance](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Sells and services life insurance policies, many part time, by visiting families and shops, collecting documents and premiums, and chasing renewals `[A]` `[G]`. LIC's ANANDA app lets an agent complete a new policy without paper after a one time eKYC, and in February 2025 LIC added One Man Office, an app for its sales force `[D]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Branch office or home | Renewal lists, calling clients `[G]` | Phone, a diary `[G]` | All day `[G]` | Clients calling back `[G]` |
| Afternoon | Clients' shops and homes | Explaining plans, collecting documents `[A]` `[G]` | Phone in hand, papers in a bag `[G]` | Photographing documents `[G]` | Clients' families `[G]` |
| Evening | Homes, the tea stall | Visits when families are home `[G]` | Standing, sometimes in the rain `[G]` | Sending photos and payment links `[G]` | Dogs, guards, lifts `[G]` |

## 3. The year
January to March has been tax saving season, since a premium only counts for the deduction under section 80C in the financial year it is paid, which ends on 31 March `[D]`; but since 2023 the new tax regime, which allows no 80C deduction, is the default, so the March rush now depends on which regime each client picks `[D]`. March closes targets `[G]`; renewals all year `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
