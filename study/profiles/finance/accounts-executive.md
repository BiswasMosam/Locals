# Accounts executive

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Finance](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Keeps the books inside one small business: vouchers in Tally, daily bank reconciliation, vendor payments by NEFT and RTGS, GST and TDS returns, petty cash `[A]` (job postings). Postings ask for a B.Com and Tally Prime; pay is about Rs 15,000 to 25,000 a month for freshers `[A]`. A good day is the bank matching the books.

## 3. The year
The 11th and 20th of every month for GST `[D]`; quarterly TDS returns `[A]`; March closing and the audit after it `[G]`; Diwali bonuses and dealer payments `[G]`.

## 4. Tools today
