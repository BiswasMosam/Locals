# Finance

People who keep the town's money in order, split into five types by **how differently their day runs**: a CA's desk ruled by portal deadlines, a student on audit at a client's office, an accounts desk inside a business, a bank counter, and an agent on the move. Specialities and seniority become the different people inside a type.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chartered accountant in practice | A small firm's office and the client's WhatsApp | Due dates on government portals | [Draft](ca-practice.md) |
| Article assistant | A client's office, on audit, and the firm's desk | The firm's deadlines and their own exams | [Draft](article-assistant.md) |
| Accounts executive | The accounts desk of a small business | Tally, the bank statement and the 20th of the month | [Draft](accounts-executive.md) |
| Bank branch officer | A public sector bank branch | The queue, the core banking screen and targets | [Draft](bank-branch.md) |
| Insurance agent | Clients' homes, shops and the tea stall | Renewals, referrals and March | [Draft](insurance-agent.md) |

## What the family shares

- **Deadlines live on government portals.** The income tax return date for 2025 moved from 31 July to 15 September, and on the last day the portal slowed so badly that the date was extended by one day, after the department had called a circulating extension notice fake the night before `[D]`. CAs and their bodies asked for October 15 and did not get it `[D]`.
- **The month has a rhythm.** GST work (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and matching input credit against GSTR-2B) is in nearly every accounts posting, with bank reconciliation and TDS returns `[A]` (job postings). GSTR-3B falls due on the 20th `[D]`.
- **March is the end of the world.** The financial year closes on 31 March: books, audits, tax saving and bank year end all land together `[D]` `[G]`.
- **Fewer people do more.** Public sector banks had 32,567 vacancies on 30 June 2025, including 12,861 clerks `[D]`, and the bank employees' union says staff cannot finish routine work within official hours `[A]`.
