# Chartered accountant in practice

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Finance](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Runs or co-runs a small firm that files returns, keeps books, audits and answers notices for traders, professionals and small companies `[A]` `[G]`. A good day is a stack of returns filed and no notice in the inbox. A bad day is the last day before a due date with the portal slowing down `[D]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Office | Reviewing staff work, signing off returns `[G]` | Two screens, a pile of files `[G]` | Client WhatsApps all morning `[G]` | Calls about notices `[G]` |
| Afternoon | Office or a client's office | Meetings, audit points, tax planning `[G]` | Laptop `[G]` | Calls between meetings `[G]` | Staff questions `[G]` |
| Evening | Office | Filing on the portals, often late near due dates `[D]` `[G]` | Laptop, OTPs on the phone `[G]` | OTPs and client documents `[G]` | Portal errors `[D]` |

## 3. The year
July to September is return season, and the 2025 date moved twice `[D]`. Tax audits close by 30 September `[D]`. GST returns every month, GSTR-3B by the 20th `[D]`. March is year end for every client at once: the tax year for everyone is the financial year from 1 April to 31 March (Income-tax Act, section 3) `[D]`.

## 4. Tools today
The income tax and GST portals, Tally files from clients, Excel, tax software, WhatsApp for documents, email `[A]` `[G]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Expert on the portals, impatient with anything that wastes a step `[G]`. Reads numbers carefully and skims everything else `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Due dates that do not move until the last day; clients who send documents late; the portal on the last day `[D]` `[G]`.
