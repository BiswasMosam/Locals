# Bank branch officer

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Finance](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Works at a public sector bank branch: the counter, account opening and KYC, loans, pensioners and complaints, with targets for selling insurance and other products on top `[A]` `[G]`. Branches are short of staff: 32,567 posts were vacant across public sector banks on 30 June 2025 `[D]`, and the officers' union says the shortage pushes officers into clerical work `[A]`.

## 3. The year
Month start brings pension and salary queues: central government pensions are credited on the last working day of each month (April's for March) `[D]`, and the queue follows `[G]`. March is year end closing `[D]`. Banks shut on the second and fourth Saturdays `[D]`; strikes and bank holidays pile up work `[A]` `[G]`.
