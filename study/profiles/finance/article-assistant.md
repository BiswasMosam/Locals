# Article assistant

**Status:** draft. Family context in [Finance](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
A CA student in two years of practical training at a CA's firm, the articleship, cut from three years in 2023 `[D]`. Sent to clients' offices on audit, does data entry, vouching and return preparation, and studies for exams on the side `[A]` `[G]`. Stipend in cities of 20 lakh people or more is at least Rs 4,000 a month in the first year and Rs 5,000 in the second `[D]`.
