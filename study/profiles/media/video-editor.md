# Video editor

**Status:** draft from thin published sources. Family context in [Media](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Edits footage into reels, YouTube videos, wedding films or ads, as a freelancer or at an agency `[G]`. The work is long stretches at a timeline broken by client revisions, usually over WhatsApp `[A]`. Tight deadlines and many rounds of revisions are the usual complaints `[A]`; fast turnarounds of a day or two are what clients pay for `[A]`. A good day is a final export approved. A bad day is "small changes" that mean recutting half the video `[G]`.

## 3. The year
Festival campaigns and wedding season both pile work onto editors weeks before the date `[D]` `[A]`.

## 4. Tools today
An editing suite, cloud links for review, WhatsApp `[A]`, and quick web tools for formats and sizes `[G]`: every platform wants its own, a reel at 9:16 and 1080 by 1920 pixels `[D]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Power user on a computer, impatient with slow or confusing tools `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
Deadlines; revision rounds; renders that fail at the last minute `[A]` `[G]`.
