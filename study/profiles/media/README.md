# Media

People who make and manage pictures, films and posts, split into five types by how their day runs.

| Type | Where the day happens | What rules it | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wedding photographer | Venues, homes, travel between them | The muhurat and the family's schedule | [Draft](wedding-photographer.md) |
| Product photographer | A small studio with lights and a white sweep | Batches, marketplace image rules, sellers in a hurry | [Draft](product-photographer.md) |
| Videographer | On location with a crew and cards full of footage | Not missing the moment, backing up every card | [Draft](videographer.md) |
| Video editor | A desk, a timeline, renders | Revisions and the publishing deadline | [Draft](video-editor.md) |
| Social media manager | A laptop and a phone that never stops | The content calendar, approvals and replies | [Draft](social-media-manager.md) |

## What the family shares

- **Seasons rule the money.** Wedding work clusters from November to February, when auspicious dates and good weather line up `[A]` (studio blogs; promotional sources). Off season is for editing and catching up `[A]`.
- **Festivals rule the posts.** Diwali shoppers start browsing about 18 days before they buy `[D]`, so festival campaigns go live weeks early, and 66% of Diwali shoppers say creators influence what they buy `[D]`.
- **Clients live on WhatsApp.** Previews, revisions and payments run through chat `[A]`. Managing many rounds of client revisions and tight deadlines are the usual complaints `[A]`.
- **Cash comes in lumps.** Income is irregular, and the busy season can bring a cash crunch of its own `[A]`.
- **Real people to ask.** Mosam is a photographer and ran a college photography club, so the first interviews for this family are close at hand.
