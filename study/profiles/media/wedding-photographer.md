# Wedding photographer

**Status:** draft from thin published sources. Family context in [Media](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Shoots weddings and family events, alone or with a small team, often for a bigger studio `[G]`. The day follows the ceremony, not a clock: haldi in the morning, the wedding at the muhurat, the reception late at night `[G]`. A good day is every moment caught and every card backed up. A bad day is a missed moment the family will ask about for years `[G]`.

## 2. The day
| Time | Where | Doing | Hands and eyes | Phone use | Interruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Bride's or groom's home | Getting ready shots, haldi `[G]` | Camera in both hands, a second body on the shoulder `[G]` | Quick replies to the family `[G]` | Relatives asking for photos `[G]` |
| Afternoon | Venue | The ceremony `[G]` | Never free `[G]` | Almost none `[G]` | Planner calls `[G]` |
| Night | Reception hall | Stage photos, family groups `[G]` | Tired, crowded `[G]` | Sends a few teaser photos on WhatsApp `[G]` | Guests in the way `[G]` |
| Late night | Home | Backs up cards, culls `[G]` | Laptop, exhausted `[A]` | Client messages `[G]` | None `[G]` |

## 3. The year
Peak from November to February; quieter in the monsoon `[A]`. Several events a day in season is common in the region `[A]`.

## 5. Phone and ability
Expert with cameras and editing software; uses the phone in short bursts between shots `[G]`.

## 6. Pressures
A missed moment; a lost card; a family wanting photos the same night; payments that arrive late `[A]`.
