# Social media manager

**Status:** draft from thin published sources. Family context in [Media](README.md).

## 1. Snapshot
Runs brands' social accounts, at an agency or in-house: a content calendar, designs from the team, approvals from clients, posting, replies and reports `[G]`. Calendars live in spreadsheets, shared calendars or scheduling tools `[A]`. Festivals drive everything: Diwali shoppers start browsing about 18 days ahead `[D]`, so festival posts are planned and approved weeks before `[A]`. A good day is everything posted on time with no angry comments. A bad day is a post going out with a mistake on a festival morning `[G]`.
